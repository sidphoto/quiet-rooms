這是什麼

Handpan Meditation Music, Soft Steel Hand Drum for Calm & Yoga

Three hours of handpan meditation music: a steel hand drum played softly with the fingertips, warm round tones with a gentle hum, in slow flowing patterns over a soft airy pad, calm and unhurried. Seven pieces in one consistent style flow into one another. Instrumental only; no vocals or spoken words.

Good for: Meditation and mindfulness, Yoga and stretching, Reading, Quiet rest.

7 pieces are joined into a unit of about 15 minutes, each change a few seconds' crossfade, then looped to 3 hours at an even level of about −16 LUFS.

Part of the Nature Room series. For moments when you want to settle down: reading, stretching, meditation or simply resting.

The music was generated with Google Gemini, then selected and arranged into one programme; the visual is an AI-generated imagined scene. No vocals or spoken words.
