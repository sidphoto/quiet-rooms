這是什麼

三小時的深度睡眠音樂合輯：緩慢安靜的夜間氛圍、疏落的輕柔鋼琴與溫潤的頌缽長音接續播放，陪你放下一天、慢慢入睡。純音樂，沒有人聲或口語。

適合的時候

  • 睡前放鬆、準備入睡
  • 腦袋停不下來的夜晚
  • 靜心與冥想
  • 安靜休息

聲音與畫面

由 3 首曲子依序銜接成約 7 分鐘的單元（每次換曲有數秒的交叉淡化），再循環延展為 3 小時，整段響度統一在約 −16 LUFS。

畫面是 AI 生成的想像場景，並非真實地點；畫面裡出現的雨、水或火光只是視覺，不代表音樂裡有這些聲音。

怎麼聽

這支屬於 Moon Room 月夜放鬆室，主題是夜間放鬆與入睡。睡前把音量調低、關掉螢幕亮光，讓它整夜播放；整段音量平穩，沒有突然變大的段落。

音樂以 Google Gemini 生成，再挑選並編排成一段節目；畫面為 AI 生成，並非真實地點的照片。影片沒有人聲或口語。

Deep Sleep Music Mix, Soft Piano & Singing Bowls

A three-hour mix of slow, quiet night music: warm ambient tones, sparse soft piano and resonant singing bowls flowing one into the next, for letting go of the day and drifting off. Instrumental only; no vocals or spoken words.

Good for: Falling asleep, Nights when your mind won't slow down, Meditation, Quiet rest.

3 pieces are joined into a unit of about 7 minutes, each change a few seconds' crossfade, then looped to 3 hours at an even level of about −16 LUFS.

The visual, an AI-generated imagined scene: A calm sea at night under a bright full moon, moonlight reflecting on gentle waves, thin clouds and dark rocky shore. What you see is not necessarily what you hear.

Part of the Moon Room series. Turn the volume low and the screen off before bed and let it play through the night; the level stays even.

The music was generated with Google Gemini, then selected and arranged into one programme; the visual is an AI-generated imagined scene. No vocals or spoken words.
