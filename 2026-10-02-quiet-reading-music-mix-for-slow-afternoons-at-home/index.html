Quiet Reading Music Mix for Slow Afternoons at Home

A three-hour mix of warm, gentle instrumentals flowing one into the next, for reading by the window, a cup of tea and an unhurried afternoon. Instrumental only; no vocals or spoken words.

Good for: Reading, Tea and afternoon rest, Journaling, Quiet time at home.

3 pieces are joined into a unit of about 5 minutes, each change a few seconds' crossfade, then looped to 3 hours at an even level of about −16 LUFS.

The visual, an AI-generated imagined scene: A sunlit window seat with soft cushions and a knitted blanket, a cup of tea and a closed book on a small wooden table, sheer curtains and a leafy plant. What you see is not necessarily what you hear.

Part of the Cozy Room series. Good as quiet background for reading, writing or working at home without pulling your attention.

The music was generated with Google Gemini, then selected and arranged into one programme; the visual is an AI-generated imagined scene. No vocals or spoken words.
