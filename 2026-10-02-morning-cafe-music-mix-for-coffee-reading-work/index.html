這是什麼

三小時的晨間咖啡館音樂合輯：多首溫暖、不急不徐的咖啡館風格純音樂接續播放，像早晨坐在灑滿陽光的窗邊座位。純音樂，沒有人聲或口語。

適合的時候

  • 早晨喝咖啡
  • 閱讀與寫作
  • 輕鬆的工作
  • 居家或店面的背景音樂

聲音與畫面

由 3 首曲子依序銜接成約 6 分鐘的單元（每次換曲有數秒的交叉淡化），再循環延展為 3 小時，整段響度統一在約 −16 LUFS。

畫面是 AI 生成的想像場景，並非真實地點；畫面裡出現的雨、水或火光只是視覺，不代表音樂裡有這些聲音。

怎麼聽

這支屬於 Cozy Room 愜意生活館，主題是閱讀、喝咖啡與居家慢時光。適合當成看書、寫字或在家工作時的背景音樂，不搶注意力。

製作說明

音樂以 Google Gemini 生成，再挑選並編排成一段節目；畫面為 AI 生成，並非真實地點的照片。影片沒有人聲或口語。

Morning Café Music Mix for Coffee, Reading & Work

A three-hour mix of warm, unhurried café-style instrumentals flowing one into the next, like a window seat in a quiet café on a sunny morning. Instrumental only; no vocals or spoken words.

Good for: Morning coffee, Reading and writing, Relaxed work, Background music at home or in a shop.

3 pieces are joined into a unit of about 6 minutes, each change a few seconds' crossfade, then looped to 3 hours at an even level of about −16 LUFS.

The visual, an AI-generated imagined scene: A sunlit wooden café table by a large window, with a steaming cup of coffee, an open blank notebook and a potted plant; empty wooden chairs in warm morning light. What you see is not necessarily what you hear.

Part of the Cozy Room series. Good as quiet background for reading, writing or working at home without pulling your attention.

The music was generated with Google Gemini, then selected and arranged into one programme; the visual is an AI-generated imagined scene. No vocals or spoken words.
