這是什麼

三小時的專注音樂：溫暖的電鋼琴、柔和的類比鋪底音色、輕巧的撥弦與若有似無的節奏。八首同一風格的曲子接續播放，平穩不搶戲。純音樂，沒有人聲或口語。

適合的時候

  • 讀書、準備考試

  • 深度工作
  • 閱讀與寫作

聲音與畫面

由 8 首曲子依序銜接成約 16 分鐘的單元（每次換曲有數秒的交叉淡化），再循環延展為 3 小時，整段響度統一在約 −16 LUFS。

畫面是 AI 生成的想像場景，並非真實地點；畫面裡出現的雨、水或火光只是視覺，不代表音樂裡有這些聲音。

怎麼聽

這支屬於 Flow Room 心流工作間，主題是長時間專注。建議開在背景、音量調到剛好聽得見就好；配合番茄鐘工作時，整段不中斷也不會有廣告式的跳接。

音樂以 Google Gemini 生成，再挑選並編排成一段節目；畫面為 AI 生成，並非真實地點的照片。影片沒有人聲或口語。

Calm Lo-Fi Rhodes Focus Music for Study & Coding

Three hours of calm focus music: warm Rhodes electric piano, soft analog pads, gentle muted plucks and a barely-there pulse. Eight pieces in one consistent style flow into one another. Instrumental only; no vocals or spoken words.

Good for: Studying, Coding, Deep work, Reading and writing.

8 pieces are joined into a unit of about 16 minutes, each change a few seconds' crossfade, then looped to 3 hours at an even level of about −16 LUFS.

The visual, an AI-generated imagined scene: A wooden desk by a rain-streaked window in the early morning, with an open blank notebook, a pencil, a mug, a potted plant and a warm desk lamp; a blurred rainy street outside. What you see is not necessarily what you hear.

Part of the Flow Room series. Keep it in the background at a low volume. It runs without abrupt changes, so it works for long Pomodoro sessions.

The music was generated with Google Gemini, then selected and arranged into one programme; the visual is an AI-generated imagined scene. No vocals or spoken words.
