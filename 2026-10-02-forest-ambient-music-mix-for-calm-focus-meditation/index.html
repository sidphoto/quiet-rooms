這是什麼

三小時的森林氛圍音樂合輯：多首平靜、帶自然感的純音樂接續播放，像清晨薄霧中的森林。純音樂，沒有人聲或口語。

聲音與畫面

由 3 首曲子依序銜接成約 7 分鐘的單元（每次換曲有數秒的交叉淡化），再循環延展為 3 小時，整段響度統一在約 −16 LUFS。

畫面是 AI 生成的想像場景，並非真實地點；畫面裡出現的雨、水或火光只是視覺，不代表音樂裡有這些聲音。

怎麼聽

這支屬於 Nature Room 自然療癒館，主題是靜心與自然氛圍。適合需要安靜下來的時候：閱讀、伸展、冥想或單純休息。

製作說明

音樂以 Google Gemini 生成，再挑選並編排成一段節目；畫面為 AI 生成，並非真實地點的照片。影片沒有人聲或口語。

Forest Ambient Music Mix for Calm Focus & Meditation

A three-hour mix of peaceful, nature-inspired ambient pieces flowing one into the next, like a forest in the early morning mist. Instrumental only; no vocals or spoken words.

Good for: Meditation, Reading, Quiet focus, Slow mornings.

3 pieces are joined into a unit of about 7 minutes, each change a few seconds' crossfade, then looped to 3 hours at an even level of about −16 LUFS.

The visual, an AI-generated imagined scene: A misty forest stream at sunrise: water running over mossy rocks, ferns and tall trees, with rays of light through the haze. What you see is not necessarily what you hear.

Part of the Nature Room series. For moments when you want to settle down: reading, stretching, meditation or simply resting.

The music was generated with Google Gemini, then selected and arranged into one programme; the visual is an AI-generated imagined scene. No vocals or spoken words.
